## 🚀 About Me 
I'm a Data Analyst who started her career in a bio lab. Watching our experiment reactions unfold in real time through a refracting telescope, then gathering and studying that data, is what made me fall in love with data. As a Data Analyst, I clean and manipulate data to answer a wide range of questions, uncover clear stories, and create actionable insights. My focus is understanding the data deeply enough to translate it for non-technical audiences, sharing insights that help businesses plan for the future. I love this career because it's a never ending learning journey.
When I'm not working with data, I am training for swim races, volunteering at local animal rescues, or reading a good fantasy book!
